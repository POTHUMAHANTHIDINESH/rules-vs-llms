# Income-tax rules for a resident salaried individual, FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26)
Source basis: Income-tax Act 1961 as amended by the Finance Act 2024. All amounts in rupees, annual.
Clause ids in [brackets] must be cited for every rupee in an answer.

[SALARY] Gross salary = taxable salary figure supplied by the taxpayer (before exemptions and deductions).
[S16ia] Standard deduction from salary: Rs 50,000 (old regime); Rs 75,000 (new regime). Never more than salary.
[S10_13A] HRA exemption (old regime only), applies only if HRA is received and rent is paid. Exempt = least of
  (i) actual HRA received, (ii) rent paid minus 10% of basic salary (basic incl. DA), (iii) 50% of basic if the
  taxpayer lives in Delhi/Mumbai/Kolkata/Chennai, else 40% of basic. Not below zero.
[S80C] Old regime only: deduction for specified investments, capped at Rs 1,50,000.
[S80CCD1B] Old regime only: additional NPS deduction, capped at Rs 50,000.
[S80D] Old regime only: health-insurance premium. Self/spouse/children: cap Rs 25,000 (Rs 50,000 if the taxpayer is
  60 or older). Parents: additional cap Rs 25,000 (Rs 50,000 if parents are senior citizens). Each cap applies separately.
[S24b] Old regime only: interest on loan for a self-occupied house, capped at Rs 2,00,000.
[R288A] Taxable income (total income) is rounded to the nearest Rs 10 (5 rounds up). Taxable income cannot be negative.
[SLAB_OLD] Old-regime slabs on taxable income. Below 60 years: up to 2,50,000 nil; 2,50,001-5,00,000 5%;
  5,00,001-10,00,000 20%; above 10,00,000 30%. Age 60 to 79: nil up to 3,00,000, then same rates.
  Age 80 and above: nil up to 5,00,000; 5,00,001-10,00,000 20%; above 10,00,000 30%.
[SLAB_NEW] New-regime (s.115BAC(1A)) slabs, all ages: up to 3,00,000 nil; 3,00,001-7,00,000 5%;
  7,00,001-10,00,000 10%; 10,00,001-12,00,000 15%; 12,00,001-15,00,000 20%; above 15,00,000 30%.
[S87A] Rebate for residents: old regime - if taxable income <= 5,00,000, rebate = lower of tax and Rs 12,500.
  New regime - if taxable income <= 7,00,000, rebate = lower of tax and Rs 25,000.
[S87A_MR] New regime only: if taxable income exceeds the rebate limit, tax payable (before surcharge and cess)
  cannot exceed the amount by which taxable income exceeds the limit (marginal relief). No such relief in old regime.
[SURCH] Surcharge on tax (after rebate), based on taxable income: above 50,00,000 10%; above 1,00,00,000 15%;
  above 2,00,00,000 25%; above 5,00,00,000 37% (old regime) but capped at 25% in the new regime.
  Marginal relief: tax plus surcharge cannot exceed (tax plus surcharge at the threshold, using the previous rate)
  plus the income above the threshold. Surcharge rounded to the nearest rupee (half up).
[CESS] Health and education cess: 4% of (tax after rebate + surcharge), rounded to the nearest rupee (half up).
[R288B] Final tax payable is rounded to the nearest Rs 10 (5 rounds up).
